I am an Applied Scientist in Amazon’s AGI team, where I work on the Multi-Modal LLM, [Nova](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/artificial-analysis_amazon-is-back-with-nova-20-a-substantial-activity-7401687639944581121-1eYo?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop&rcm=ACoAAC4vZ_8BfaGJbQ_dVmO0fNAfqZlJ1tuKRj0). I received my Ph.D. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from the University of California, San Diego, under the supervision of Prof. [Truong Q. Nguyen](https://jacobsschool.ucsd.edu/faculty/profile?id=48). Prior to UCSD, I earned my bachelor’s and master’s degrees in Electrical Engineering from the University of Science and Technology of China, supervised by Prof.  [Dong Liu](https://faculty.ustc.edu.cn/dongeliu/en/index/85593/list/index.htm). My research interests span low-level computer vision, image and video generation, and computer vision for retinal image analysis.
